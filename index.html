What happens after you open escrow

You open the file

Send us the signed purchase agreement and the contact details for both sides.

We gather and verify

Deposit, title, lender requirements and disclosures are collected in one place.

Everyone signs

We schedule signing and review the documents against the written instructions.

We close and disburse

Once conditions are met, funds go out, the deed is recorded and you are done.

Straight answers

Short versions of what people ask us most. Call for anything specific to your file.

What does an escrow officer actually do?

We act as the neutral third party. We receive the deposit, documents and instructions from each side, make sure the conditions of the agreement are met, and then release funds and documents to the right people.

When should I open escrow?

As soon as you have a signed purchase agreement or loan commitment. Opening early gives us time to catch problems before they threaten your closing date.

What will it cost?

Fees depend on the size and type of the transaction. Call or email and we will give you a clear estimate for your situation.
